Category Details & What to Expect
1. Conchas Chinas Setting & Views
("The Beverly Hills of Puerto Vallarta")		 Perched at the highest point of gated Upper Conchas Chinas (Paseo de los Corales 139) where the Sierra Madre jungle meets the bay, commanding unobstructed 180°–270° views over Banderas Bay, Los Arcos marine rocks, and city lights. In late Dec / early Jan, you can spot breaching humpback whales right from your private balcony with morning coffee.
Distances: 5–8 mins ($3–$5 Uber) to Zona Romántica & Los Muertos Pier • 5 mins down the hill to Conchas Chinas beach coves • 25–30 mins to PVR airport.

• Living, Dining & Kitchen: Central open-concept living room, 6-person indoor dining table, full kitchen (full-size fridge, cooktop, microwave, blender, coffee maker, tableware), and wide covered ocean-view terrace.

(4 Heated Pools + 3 Jacuzzis)		 • Main Family Pool Level: 3 interconnected heated infinity pools, shallow sun shelf, and heated whirlpool overlooking Banderas Bay with poolside food/drink service from La Terraza.
• The Rooftop Level (Sunset Deck): Sky-high infinity pool + 2 heated Jacuzzis on the top floor with panoramic 360° bay and mountain sunset views.
• The View Spa & Gym: Full hydrotherapy circuit (steam room, sauna, Jacuzzi), massage cabanas, salon, and ocean-view fitness center.
4. Dining (On-Site & Zona Romántica) • On-Site: Cielito Lindo (open-air Mexican/American breakfast & dinner), Eugenia Restaurant & Rooftop (elevated fine dining & sunset mixology), La Terraza Snack Bar (poolside ceviches, aguachiles, fish tacos), and The Coffee Bar.

5. Honest Pros vs. Tradeoffs
(vs. Garza Blanca ~$11,580)		 Pros:
• ~$3,500 Savings: At ~$8,080 all-in, leaves nearly $4,000 under the $12,000 budget for fine dining and excursions.
• Boutique Calm (~100 Suites): Intimate, uncrowded pools without the 7 AM mega-resort lounger rush.
• Closer to Town: 5–8 mins to Zona Romántica vs. 15–20 mins south from Garza Blanca.

• Smaller Footprint than Garza Blanca: 1,720 sq ft (2BR/2BA) on a hillside terrace vs. Garza Blanca's 3,229 sq ft (2BR/3BA) on a private beachfront cove.

Category Details & What to Expect
1. Flamingos Beach Setting (Riviera Nayarit)
(Wide, Flat 3-Mile Walkable Golden-Sand Beach)		 Located north of the airport in Flamingos / Nuevo Nayarit (right next door to AAA Four-Diamond sister resort Villa La Estancia), sitting directly on one of the widest, flattest, most swimmable golden-sand beaches on Banderas Bay.
• No Hills or Steep Stairs: Unlike Conchas Chinas (Grand Miramar) or the South Shore cliffs (Garza Blanca), the entire resort and beach are completely flat—you walk straight from the elevator past the lagoon pool onto miles of soft sand with gentle waves, complimentary kayaks/paddleboards, and palapa loungers.
• Distances: 5–8 mins ($4–$6 taxi/Uber, or a 25-min beach walk north) to the charming cobblestone beach town of Bucerías (La Postal, Karen's Place, Mar y Sol, art walk & taco stands) • 20–25 mins to PVR airport • 35–45 mins to Downtown PV / Zona Romántica.

5. Honest Pros vs. Tradeoffs
(Middle-Ground Pick at ~$9,980 Total)		 Pros:
• Sweet-Spot Price (~$9,980 All-In): Sits $2,000+ under the $12,000 budget (~$1,600 cheaper than Garza Blanca) while giving you true step-out beachfront access.
• Best Walkable Beach + 3 Full Bathrooms + Laundry: Miles of flat golden sand (ideal for morning walks and swimming), 1 King + 2 Queens + 3 full bathrooms, and an in-suite washer/dryer.
• Close to Bucerías & PVR Airport: Only 20 mins from the airport and 5 mins from authentic Bucerías.

Tradeoffs to Know Ahead of Time:
• Traditional 4-Star Timeshare Finishes (Closer to Pueblo Bonito): Built by The Villa Group (same developer as Villa del Palmar Cabo), the travertine/dark-wood interiors and 1,309 sq ft footprint feel very similar to Pueblo Bonito—comfortable and family-friendly, but not ultra-modern 5-star luxury like Garza Blanca (3,229 sq ft) or right next door at sister property Villa La Estancia (2,163 sq ft, ~$11,680).
• 35–45 Mins from Downtown PV / Zona Romántica: Great if you want to stay on Flamingos Beach and dine in Bucerías, but a longer taxi ride if you plan to go into downtown Puerto Vallarta multiple nights.
