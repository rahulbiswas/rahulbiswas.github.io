• 1990s Exterior Architecture & Darker Marina Sand: While the suite interiors are nicely renovated, the building exteriors are classic 1990s condo-resort architecture, and Marina Vallarta's beach sand is darker and slightly coarser than Garza Blanca's white-sand cove or Flamingos Beach.
