Marrakesh: The Final 3 Riads (Thu June 17 – Sun June 20, 2027)

The final 3 from the 9 riads on the wishlist, checked against their live Airbnb listings for 3 nights, 2 adults + 2 kids (13 & 6). Sorted from lowest to highest price. All 3 come in under the $2,700 budget, and every price includes all fees.

Arrive: BA 2812 lands RAK 6:15 PM Thu Budget: $2,700 ($900/night) Range: $1,160 – $2,135 Next: ONCF train to Fès, Sun 8:35 AM

How to choose

  • Boutique Riad Medina ($1,160) if you want the safest bet for the least money: 268 reviews at 4.96 and a concierge who walks you in. 3 bedrooms.
  • Riad Kaolane ($1,270) if you want 4 bedrooms, the closest spot to Jemaa el-Fna, and guaranteed check-in until 10 PM. Only 11 reviews.
  • Dar Num ($2,135) if you want the most space and polish: 320 m², big rooms, 128 reviews at 4.99. Confirm an ~8 PM welcome.

What applies to all 3

  • AC: all 3 list air conditioning. That matters in mid-June, when it's 35–38°C (95–100°F).
  • Pools: riad pools are small plunge pools with no fence or gate. Keep an eye on Ellie.
  • Medina access: cars stop at the nearest lane. Staff meet you there, which is easy with carry-ons.
  • Arrival: you land at 6:15 PM, so expect to reach the riad around 7:30–8 PM. Book the airport transfer through the riad.
