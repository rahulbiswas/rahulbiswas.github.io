Marrakesh: The 9 Saved Riads (Thu June 17 – Sun June 20, 2027)

All 9 places from the wishlist, checked against their live Airbnb listings for 3 nights, 2 adults + 2 kids (13 & 6). Sorted from lowest to highest price. All 9 come in under the $2,700 budget, and every price includes all fees.

Arrive: BA 2812 lands RAK 6:15 PM Thu Budget: $2,700 ($900/night) Range: $884 – $2,188 Next: ONCF train to Fès, Sun 8:35 AM

Top 3 for the four of you

  • Dar Num ($2,135): the best riad here. 320 m², 4 big en-suite rooms, 128 reviews at 4.99, staff 8–5, parking nearby. $565 under budget.
  • Dar Madjoul ($1,517): 4 bedrooms and 4.5 baths by Bahia Palace for about $600 less. Ask the host about arriving after 8 PM.
  • Boutique Riad Medina ($1,160): best value. 268 reviews at 4.96, 3 en-suite queens, and the concierge walks you in.

What applies to all 9

  • AC: all 9 list air conditioning. That matters in mid-June, when it's 35–38°C (95–100°F).
  • Pools: riad pools are small plunge pools with no fence or gate. Keep an eye on Ellie.
  • Medina access: cars stop at the nearest lane. Staff meet you there, which is easy with carry-ons. Only Dar Louma lets the car pull up to the door.
  • Arrival: you land at 6:15 PM, so expect to reach the riad around 7:30–8 PM. Book the airport transfer through the riad.
