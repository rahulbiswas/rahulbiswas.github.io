1. Historic Identity & Architectural Pedigree

Tudor Foundation & 1712 Queen Anne Rebuild

  • Grade II* Listed Status: Placed in the top 5.8% of all protected historic buildings in England (a higher architectural tier than standard Grade II).
  • Sir John Harington Connection (16th Century): Built over the 16th-century vaulted stone basement of a Tudor manor owned by Sir John Harington—godson to Queen Elizabeth I and inventor of the flushing water closet.
  • 1712 Golden Bath-Stone Facade: Rebuilt in 1712 in symmetrical Queen Anne style for High Sheriff Henry Walters, featuring segment-headed sash windows, Corinthian pilasters, and a pedimented Ionic doorcase.

Royal History & Museum-Grade Restoration

  • Princess Beatrice's Music Room: Later home to noted pianist Francesca Ferrari, who taught Queen Victoria's youngest daughter, Princess Beatrice, to play the piano in the drawing room.
  • Watson, Bertram & Fell Restoration: Meticulously restored by the acclaimed Bath classical architecture firm across four stories, combining period wall panelling, working fireplaces, and antique chandeliers with underfloor-heated flagstones, high-speed Wi-Fi, and Sonos audio.
  • 6 Named Historic Bedrooms: Every bedroom is named after a historical figure tied to the estate (Harington, Walters, Blanchard, Pagden, Trevenan, Marsh).

6. Three Fine-Print Caveats to Weigh Before Booking

Caveat 1 — Southern Cotswolds / Bath Edge vs. North Cotswolds Villages: Batheaston House is a 10–15 minute drive from historic Bath and 20 minutes from Castle Combe and Lacock, making it unbeatable if you want to pair Georgian Bath with the southern Cotswolds. However, the famous North/Central Cotswolds villages (Bourton-on-the-Water, Lower Slaughter, Stow-on-the-Wold) are 1 hr 10 mins to 1 hr 20 mins north by car. (By comparison, #2 Spring House is 25 minutes from Bourton-on-the-Water.)
Caveat 2 — Separate Annexe & Gardener Disclosure in the Listing Rules: In the "Other things to note" section, host Paul discloses: "There is also an annexe in a separate building, with its own entrance that can be booked in addition to the main house, or separately by other guests. The owner may occasionally be staying in the annexe from time to time. The gardener may also be working in the grounds during your stay."
Caveat 3 — Children Setting on Airbnb (Quick Host Message Needed): Because the 1.15-acre grounds include an ornamental courtyard pond, stone terrace steps, and direct River Avon bank access at the foot of the garden, Airbnb's automated guest selector flags the listing with: "The host indicated their place has features that aren’t kid-friendly. You can message the host for more details if you’re traveling with kids." As Review #10 (Yolanda, Jan 2026) confirms, Paul happily hosts families with children once you message him on Airbnb.
