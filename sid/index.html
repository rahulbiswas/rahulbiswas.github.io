About me

I'm Sid. I build things when a question gets stuck in my head and won't leave.

It usually starts small and gets out of hand. I wanted to know whether a computer could beat me at Dou Shou Qi, so I wrote one that does. I wanted to know whether Santa Clara County is really getting hotter or whether it just feels that way every August, so I pulled decades of NOAA records and plotted them. Then California had a brutal 2024, and it turns out ECOSTRESS thermal imaging is a remarkably direct way to watch a heatwave happen from orbit.

The newest one is StockTest. Everybody has a strategy that would have beaten the market. I wanted to check mine honestly — with taxes, with trading costs, against a benchmark that doesn't get to cheat. It runs entirely in your browser: I ship the price history with the page, so there's no server, no account, and nothing you do ever leaves your laptop.

Mostly Python, TypeScript and React. My favorite part is always the moment the thing finally runs and immediately tells me something I didn't expect.
